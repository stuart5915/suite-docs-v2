# 🌱 Staking SUITE

**Lock tokens to earn yield and voting power**

---

## How Staking Works

1. Connect wallet at [getsuite.app/wallet.html](https://getsuite.app/wallet.html)
2. Choose amount to stake
3. Select lock duration
4. Confirm transaction
5. Start earning yield

---

## Staking Tiers

| Duration | Yield Boost | Voting Power |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| 30 days | 1.0x | 1.0x |
| 90 days | 1.5x | 1.25x |
| 180 days | 2.0x | 1.5x |
| 365 days | 3.0x | 2.0x |

---

## Yield Sources

Staking yield comes from:

- **Ecosystem fees** — Portion of all transactions
- **Treasury growth** — As TVL increases
- **Boost premiums** — App boost payments

---

## Unstaking

- **Before lock expires:** Early withdrawal penalty (varies by duration)
- **After lock expires:** Withdraw anytime, no penalty
- **Auto-compound:** Option to re-stake rewards automatically

---

## Governance Benefits

Staked SUITE gives you:

- **Voting rights** on ecosystem proposals
- **Giving Fund votes** to direct charitable allocations
- **Early access** to new features
- **Reviewer eligibility** for app approvals
