# 💵 Revenue & Fees

**Understanding how money flows through SUITE**

---

## Fee Structure

| Transaction Type | Total Fee | Developer | Treasury | Giving |
|------------------|-----------|-----------|----------|--------|
| App Purchase | 30% | 70% | 20% | 10% |
| Content Unlock | 20% | 80% | 15% | 5% |
| Subscription | 25% | 75% | 18% | 7% |
| Boost Payment | 100% | 0% | 90% | 10% |

---

## Where Fees Go

### 🏦 Treasury (20%)
- Increases ETH backing for all SUITE
- Raises floor price over time
- Strengthens ecosystem stability

### ❤️ Giving Fund (10%)
- Community-directed charitable giving
- Quarterly allocation votes
- Transparent on-chain tracking

---

## Example Transaction

User pays **100 SUITE** for premium feature:

```
User: -100 SUITE
   ├── Developer: +70 SUITE
   ├── Treasury: +20 SUITE (stays as ETH backing)
   └── Giving Fund: +10 SUITE
```

---

## Payout Timing

- **Developers:** Instant (on-chain transaction)
- **Stakers:** Daily (auto-compound available)
- **Giving Fund:** Quarterly distributions
