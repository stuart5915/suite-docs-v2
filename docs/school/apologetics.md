## How It Works

**1. Submit a Challenge:** Pose an argument or question challenging the Christian faith. Pay SUITE tokens to submit (tokens go to the Giving Fund).

**2. AI Refutation:** The AI Judge analyzes your challenge and provides a refutation based on Scripture, logic, and the existing database of responses.

**3. Novelty Check:** If your challenge is truly novel — something the ecosystem hasn't encountered before — you earn a reward from the Treasury.

**4. Database Grows:** Every challenge and refutation is added to the visible database, making the system stronger and the bar for novelty higher.

---

## What Defines "Novel"?

A challenge is considered novel if:

- It is **semantically distinct** from all existing challenges in the database
- It raises a **genuinely new angle** not covered by existing refutations
- The AI Judge determines it requires **new reasoning** to address

*As the database grows, novelty becomes increasingly difficult to achieve — like mining for truth. This is by design.*

## What If the AI Can't Refute?

If a challenge is so profound that the AI cannot immediately refute it:

- The challenge is **flagged for deep study**
- Human researchers (Stuart + community) investigate
- A proper response is generated and added to the database
- The submitter receives a **premium reward** for advancing the conversation

:::success
This has never happened. Because truth wins. But the possibility keeps the system honest.
:::
