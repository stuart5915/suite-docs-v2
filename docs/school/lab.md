## What Is The Lab?

The SUITE Lab is the **contemplative engine** of the ecosystem. It's where we explore questions too big for immediate implementation — questions about God's Will, energy dynamics, the future of technology, and how SUITE should evolve.

The Lab uses AI to extend human thinking, generating **daily intuitions** that help guide ecosystem decisions. Think of it as a research arm that feeds back into governance and roadmap.
