# 🌐 SUITE Ecosystem Overview

**The complete guide to everything in SUITE — apps, tokens, earning, and community.**

---

## The SUITE Journey

Everyone enters SUITE at their own level. Whether you're just discovering us or ready to build the next great app, there's a path for you.

### 🌱 Discovery Phase
Just learning about SUITE? Start here:
- Explore the [SUITE Bible](/the-suite-bible) — our philosophy and values
- Browse the [App Store](/apps.html) — see what's being built
- Join [SUITE Hub](https://discord.gg/suite) — meet the community

### 🔧 Builder Phase
Ready to create? Level up:
- Use [Cadence AI](/build/ai-fleet) — describe apps in plain English
- Deploy through [FORGE](/learn/systems-view) — our Discord bot handles the rest
- Earn $SUITE when users pay for your apps

### 🚀 Contributor Phase
Going deeper? Become essential:
- Stake SUITE to become an [Incubator](/incubate.html)
- Review and approve community apps
- Earn from the [Giving Fund](/learn/governance)

## Core Components

### 🏦 Vault & Treasury
The economic heart of SUITE:
- **Deposit ETH** → Mint $SUITE tokens
- **Burn SUITE** → Withdraw proportional ETH
- **Floor Price Guarantee** — SUITE is always backed by real ETH

### 🤖 AI Fleet
Autonomous app creation system:
- **TELOS Mode** — AI builds apps 24/7 without human input
- **Cadence AI** — Natural language to working code
- **FORGE Bot** — Discord commands for app management

### 📱 App Store
Where apps live:
- Community-built mobile apps
- Powered by Expo for iOS/Android
- Monetized through $SUITE microtransactions

### ❤️ Giving Fund
Purpose beyond profit:
- 10% of all transaction fees
- Community votes on recipients
- Transparent on-chain allocation
