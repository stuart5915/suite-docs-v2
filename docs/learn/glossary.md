# 🗣️ SUITE Lingo

**A glossary of terms and concepts in the SUITE ecosystem**

---

## Core Concepts

### SUITE Token
The utility token used across all apps. Backed by ETH in the Treasury.

### Treasury
Smart contract holding ETH that backs all SUITE tokens. Guarantees a floor price.

### Floor Price
The minimum value of SUITE = Treasury ETH ÷ Total Supply. You can always redeem at this rate.

---

## Agents & Systems

### Cadence
The AI that generates code from natural language prompts.

### FORGE
Discord bot for managing prompts, apps, and receiving notifications.

### Watcher
Background service that monitors for new prompts and syncs code.

### AI Fleet
The collective of automated systems (Cadence, FORGE, Watcher) working together.

### TELOS
Autonomous goal-seeking mode where AI picks and builds apps independently.

---

## Actions

### Mint
Deposit ETH → Receive SUITE tokens at current exchange rate.

### Burn
Send SUITE tokens → Receive proportional ETH from Treasury.

### Stake
Lock SUITE tokens for yield and governance voting power.

### Boost
Pay to increase app visibility in the App Store.

---

## Places

### App Store
Marketplace where users discover and use SUITE apps.

### Dashboard
Control center for managing apps and AI Fleet configuration.

### Vault
Treasury interface for depositing/withdrawing ETH.

### Giving Fund
10% of ecosystem fees allocated to charity via community voting.

---

## Roles

### Developer
Creates apps using Cadence AI or manual coding.

### Reviewer
Approves apps for the App Store.

### Staker
Locks SUITE for yield and voting rights.

### Admin
Has elevated permissions for ecosystem management.
