# 🗳️ Governance

**How the SUITE community makes decisions together**

---

## Governance Overview

SUITE uses token-based governance. Staked SUITE = voting power.

---

## What Can Be Voted On?

### Giving Fund Allocations
Community decides which charities receive funds from the 10% Giving allocation.

### Ecosystem Proposals
Major changes to:
- Fee structures
- Token mechanics
- Feature priorities
- Policy changes

### App Approvals
Community reviewers vote on whether apps meet quality standards.

---

## Voting Power

```
Voting Power = Staked SUITE × Time Multiplier
```

Longer staking = higher multiplier. This rewards long-term commitment.

| Stake Duration | Multiplier |
|----------------|------------|
| 30 days | 1.0x |
| 90 days | 1.25x |
| 180 days | 1.5x |
| 365 days | 2.0x |

---

## Proposal Process

1. **Draft** — Submit proposal with rationale
2. **Discussion** — Community feedback period (7 days)
3. **Voting** — Token-weighted voting (3 days)
4. **Execution** — If passed, changes are implemented

---

## Philosopher King Transition

Initially, the founder (Stuart) retains final say on major decisions. Over time, power transitions to the community:

| Phase | Governance |
|-------|------------|
| Phase 1 | Founder decides, community advises |
| Phase 2 | Community votes, founder can veto |
| Phase 3 | Full community governance |
