## The Method: Ontological Design

How does one generate new incentive systems, new connections, new worlds — all founded in coherent meaning? Here is the process:

### 1. Externalize the Implicit
Take what exists only in your mind and make it visible. Write down every entity, every concept, every named thing.

### 2. Contemplate with Stillness
Sit with your externalized map. Don't force connections. Let patterns emerge in an atmosphere of intense potential.

### 3. Draw New Connections
As you see the whole, new relationships reveal themselves. These intuitions are the generative engine of the system.

### 4. Name the New Things
When you see a pattern that needs a name, name it. TELOS. FORGE. Cadence. These aren't just labels — they're invocations.

### 5. Recurse
The new names create new space for more naming. Return to step one with your expanded ontology. Each cycle adds coherence.
